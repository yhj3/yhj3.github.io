Yihang Jiao

Hi! I'm Yihang Jiao, a Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I also received my B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science. Previously I was a research assistant at FOCAL Lab with Prof. Gagandeep Singh, working on the robustness of neural language models.

My research interests lie in:

  • AI for Bio: I want to build models that survive contact with real biomedical data, where labels are scarce, positives are rare, and a good average can hide a bad result on the case that actually matters.
  • AI Safety: I am interested in how language models behave when someone is deliberately trying to break them, and in what that reveals about what they have really learned.
  • AI Agents: I enjoy building LLM agents for concrete tasks — repairing real bugs, finding the right paper — and then checking honestly whether they work.

Running through all three is evaluation, which feels a lot like grading a student's work: a model can reach the right answer for the wrong reason, and unless the test is built to tell the difference, the score flatters everyone.

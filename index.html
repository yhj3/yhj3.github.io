Yihang Jiao

Hi! I'm Yihang Jiao, a Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I also received my B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science. Previously I was a research assistant at FOCAL Lab with Prof. Gagandeep Singh, working on the robustness of neural language models.

My research interests lie in:

  • AI for Bio: building models for biomedical data, where the labels are few and the cases that matter are rare.
  • AI Safety: testing what language models do when someone is trying to break them on purpose.
  • AI Agents: building agents for concrete tasks, such as repairing bugs or finding the right paper, and then testing whether they work.

Most of my work has been about how these models are measured. I have built segmentation pipelines, retrieval systems, and the evaluations that check them.

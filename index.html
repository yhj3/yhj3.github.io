Yihang Jiao

Hi! I'm Yihang Jiao, a Master of Computer Science student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I also received my B.S. in Mathematics and Computer Science. Previously I was a research assistant at FOCAL Lab with Prof. Gagandeep Singh, working on the robustness of neural language models.

My research interests lie in:

  • AI for Bio: I want to build models for biomedical data, where labels are scarce, positives are rare, and a good average score can hide a bad result on the case that matters most.
  • AI Safety: I am interested in how language models behave when someone is deliberately trying to break them, because that is when we learn what they have actually learned.
  • AI Agents: I enjoy building language-model agents for concrete tasks, such as repairing real bugs or finding the right paper, and then testing honestly whether they work.

What connects the three is evaluation. In each of my projects the most important finding came from the measurement rather than from the model. A model being confident is very different from a model being right, and a benchmark can easily confirm itself if we do not question how it is built.

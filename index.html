Selected Work

Brain Tumor Segmentation on Multi-Modal MRI: U-Net vs. TransUNet

2025
Independent project · BraTS-style 2D pipeline, four MRI modalities

A single training entrypoint for three model families (U-Net, configurable U-Net, ResNet50 + ViT TransUNet) under identical data and optimization settings, so architectural effects can be isolated. TransUNet reached 0.7575 foreground Dice vs. 0.7326 for the tuned U-Net — but the transformer's advantage was localized to the diffuse tumor subregion (0.7652 vs. 0.6800), not to compact structures. Redesigning the objective alone recovered roughly +7 Dice points on an unchanged architecture.

VibeRepair+: Retrieval-Augmented Program Repair for Java and Python

2026
CS Software Engineering for GenAI · with Yingjia Gu and Shengyue Guo

A pluggable retriever framework over a 97,402-example bug-fix corpus, plus a correction to how these systems are evaluated. The incumbent leave-one-out protocol reports a perfect recall@1 for every retriever — while those same retrievers agree on only 34.1% of top-1 results. Under an external-query protocol on 521 Defects4J bugs, lexical BM25 beat the dense baseline by +27.8% relative recall@10, inverting the usual dense-beats-lexical assumption.

LLM Reranking for Scientific Literature with Citation Context

2024
CS 598 Text Mining · co-author

Reranking candidate papers through LLM-adjudicated pairwise comparisons, treating noisy LLM judgments as a dueling-bandit problem: UCB elimination cuts 190 exhaustive comparisons at K=20 to roughly 44. Augmenting candidates with in-link citation context — from a corpus of 10,395 papers and 497,210 citation contexts — raised MRR from 0.485 to 0.572 at K=5.

Research Experience

FOCAL Lab, UIUC — Research Assistant

Aug 2024 – Dec 2024
Robustness of neural language models · advised by Prof. Gagandeep Singh
  • Built a hybrid adversarial-attack framework pairing LLaMA-2 counterfactual generation with a three-stage semantic filter (label-flip rate ≥ 0.6, embedding cosine similarity ≥ 0.75, GPT-2-XL perplexity ≤ 150), probing classifier robustness beyond word-substitution attacks.
  • Filtering discarded 37.7% of generated candidates while retaining 91% of effective adversarial examples, raising attack success from 68.2% to 85.1% on RoBERTa-Large and 71.4% to 79.8% on BERT-Base over TextFooler and PWWS baselines.
  • Found susceptibility scales with model capacity, and that inference-style tasks (RTE, QNLI; +11.3% average) gain more from generative rewriting than sentiment tasks, where lexical substitution already suffices.
